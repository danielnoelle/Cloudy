How I work.

Four phases. Every build moves from a systems problem to something dependable in production.
01

Understand

Clarify the problem, constraints, users, and the failure modes worth designing around before touching the implementation.

02

Design

Map the architecture, workflows, and interface. I choose the smallest reliable shape that can grow with the work.

03

Implement

Build the solution, connect the tools, and test each workflow so the result is secure, usable, and ready for everyday work.

04

Operate

Ship, monitor, document, and improve. The job continues after launch because dependable systems need care.
